Behavioral Assessment

A practical team operating tool. Understand how you naturally work, how you fit with your teammates, and how your manager can get the best from you.

~6 minutes Two questions · Same word list 7 behavioral profiles Manager playbook included

Before you begin

  • You will see the same list of words twice, with a different question each time.
  • Select the words that feel genuinely true — don't overthink it.
  • If you don't recognize a word, skip it.
  • Please complete it in one sitting.
  • There are no right or wrong answers.

Your result will be saved and visible to your manager in the Team view.

This assessment is an internal behavioral awareness tool designed to support team development and communication. It is not a clinical or psychological evaluation, and results are not used as a basis for hiring, promotion, compensation, or any employment decision. Participation is voluntary. Your responses and profile are stored securely and accessible only to you and your manager.

Your data is processed for internal team development purposes only and will not be shared outside Logiztik Alliance Group.
